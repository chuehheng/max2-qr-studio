# MAX2 QR Studio

A standalone GoPro MAX2 360° camera settings and QR code tool.

## Use

Open the published page in a modern browser, choose compatible camera settings, then scan the generated QR code with a MAX2 running GoPro Labs firmware.

The app runs entirely in the browser. Saved presets stay in that browser unless exported.

## Notes

- 8K 360 supports 24, 25, and 30 fps; 5.6K supports 24, 25, 30, 50, and 60 fps.
- Labs custom bitrate and 10-bit are offered for 8K 360.
- All-I is experimental and requires 8K with at least 180 Mbps selected.
- Camera behavior can vary by firmware and recording conditions; verify settings on the camera.

This is an independent tool and is not affiliated with GoPro.
